## What this guide helps you do

The aim is to focus your preparation on questions that actually appear in interviews. There are hundreds of possible theory questions, so prioritizing matters. This guide contains more than 300 questions; according to the source statistics, 85 appear in at least one in ten interviews.

Each [technical question](questions.md) shows how frequently it appears in the community's interview recordings. These figures are calculated automatically from the recordings' timestamps. As of June 11, 2026, the source collection contained 287 interviews.

## What is included

- [Technical questions and answers](questions.md) across Java, Spring, microservices, databases, testing, deployment, and related topics, with interview-frequency figures. All 22 technical categories are available in Russian and English.
- [Work experience questions](https://zhukovsd.github.io/java-backend-interview-prep/work-experience/) (in Russian), which the source reports appear in 90% of interviews.
- [Thought exercises](https://zhukovsd.github.io/java-backend-interview-prep/thought-exercises/) (in Russian), appearing in 62% of the source interviews.
- [Live coding tasks](https://zhukovsd.github.io/java-backend-interview-prep/livecoding/) (in Russian) covering Java, SQL, Stream API, and refactoring, appearing in 51% of the source interviews.

## How to use the guide

1. Work through the topics in the order shown in the [technical questions list](questions.md).
2. Within each topic, start with questions appearing in at least 5–10% of interviews. Investigate the concepts and write your own notes instead of memorizing answers.
3. Visit the [community chat](https://t.me/+8YGzqvJOndg1Yjky) and ask for a mock interview on one or more topics.
4. After covering the theory, practice with thought exercises and live coding tasks.

Use **Русский / English** above the page to change languages. The selected language is retained as you move between translated topics, and the browser remembers your preference when storage is available.
